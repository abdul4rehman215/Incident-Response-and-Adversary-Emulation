OSINT Legal Compliance Checklist

Before conducting OSINT activities:

□ Ensure you have proper authorization
□ Only target domains you own or have permission to test
□ Respect robots.txt and terms of service
□ Do not exceed reasonable request rates
□ Document your activities for compliance
□ Avoid accessing private or restricted information
□ Follow organizational security policies
□ Consider data protection regulations (GDPR, etc.)

Safe Practice Domains for Testing:
• example.com (IANA reserved)
• test.com (if owned by your organization)
• Your own registered domains

Remember:
OSINT should only collect publicly available information.
